# Problem Statement

## Background
The company is seeing increased customer churn. The Growth team wants to identify customers at high risk of churning so they can prioritize retention campaigns.

## Objective
Build a churn intelligence pipeline that:
1. predicts which customers are likely to churn,
2. identifies key churn drivers,
3. estimates business value of targeting high-risk customers.

## Stakeholders
- Growth Manager
- CRM Team
- Customer Success Team
- Analytics Manager

## Target Variable
Churn = customer leaves the service.

## Business Questions
- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- Which segments have the highest churn rate?
- What is the expected retained revenue if the company targets the top-risk customers?

## Constraints
- Limited retention budget
- Class imbalance
- Potential data leakage
